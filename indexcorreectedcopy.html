  • Jujutsu Kaisen 

    The story of Jujutsu Kaisen is set in a world where cursed spirits feed on unsuspecting humans and fragments of the legendary and feared demon Ryomen Sukuna have been lost and scattered about. Should any curse consume Sukuna's fingers, the power they gain could destroy the world as we know it. Fortunately, there exists a mysterious school of jujutsu sorcerers who exist to protect the precarious existence of the living from the supernatural!

  • Solo Leveling 

    Solo Leveling, also alternatively translated as Only I Level Up (Korean: 나 혼자만 레벨업), is a South Korean fantasy web novel written by Chugong. It was serialized in Kakao's digital comic and fiction platform KakaoPage beginning on July 25, 2016, and was later published by D&C Media under their Papyrus label since November 4, 2016.

A webtoon adaptation of Solo Leveling was first serialized in KakaoPage on March 4, 2018. It was illustrated by Jang Sung-rak (Dubu). An anime television series adaptation produced by A-1 Pictures aired in 2024, followed by a second season in 2025. A sequel anime film, "Beyond the System", has also been announced.

  • Demon Slayer 

    Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Koyoharu Gotouge. It follows Tanjiro Kamado, who joins the Demon Slayer Corps after his family is slaughtered and his sister Nezuko is turned into a demon.

The anime adaptation by Ufotable became one of the most successful anime franchises ever, with multiple seasons and blockbuster movies including Mugen Train and the Infinity Castle trilogy.
